## What this section is

These are the Espressif boards used as custom nodes under the dome. Each page below is the hardware reference for one board: chip, pins, power, and the quirks that show up when you actually wire it.

How those nodes are flashed, and the MQTT topics they publish, lives under [Firmware](/iot/firmware/esphome/general-guide) — ESPHome for the configs, [MQTT conventions](/iot/firmware/standards/mqtt-conventions) for the topic and payload schema.

## Not an ESP32

The [ESP8266 IdeaSpark](/iot/mcus/esp8266-ideaspark/hardware-ref) is the older board, kept for basic sensor nodes with a built-in OLED. It sits next to this section under MCUs, not in the ESP32 family.
